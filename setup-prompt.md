# Setup prompt

Create a bot called Chief Of Staff, paste this into its chat, and answer its question.

```text
Set up my Grok Bot executive team from the grokbot-executive-team repo.

1. Read README.md, guides/rules.md, guides/multi-project-mode.md and every
   file in agents/.
2. Ask me one question, then wait: "One company, or several projects?"
   (If I say several, I'll list them, one line each on what they do.)
3. Take on the Chief Of Staff instructions from agents/01-chief-of-staff.md.
   I am the CEO/exec at the top; you are the hub under me.
4. Create the department heads (CTO/Product, CFO/Finance, CIO/Ops & IT,
   CMO), the CMO's marketing team (SEO/GEO, Social Media, Content, Paid Ads,
   Marketing Analyst, Growth & Partnerships, Email & Lifecycle) and the
   supporting bots (Creative Director, LinkedIn Outreach, Specialist /
   Research), each with the instructions block from its file. Keep every
   standing rule exactly as strict as written.
5. Only if I said several projects: also create one "<Project> CEO" bot per
   project from agents/optional-project-ceo.md, replacing <Project> with
   each name, reporting to you.
6. Create these routines, and no others:
   - Chief Of Staff: daily briefing, Sunday-Friday about 8am my time, none
     on Saturday (Shabbat). Decisions at the top, what's going on, what's
     next, 5 growth ideas, combined from each department head (and each
     Project CEO in multi-project mode).
   - CFO: monthly expense report on the 1st, per department or project.
7. Use the time zone from my account settings; ask only if it isn't set.
8. Show me what you created and anything I must finish in the app.

During setup do not connect tools, send messages or spend money.
```
